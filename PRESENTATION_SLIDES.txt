SLIDE 7 — VS. GLOFOX, MINDBODY & OFF-THE-SHELF PLATFORMS
============================================================

"But can't we just use Glofox or Mindbody?"
Let's break down the REAL cost. There are 3 types of fees:

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

FEE #1: MONTHLY BASE FEE (the subscription to use their software)
  • Glofox charges $110–$250+/month just to log in and use it
  • This is a flat fee you pay whether you have 5 members or 500
  • Think of it like rent — you pay it just to exist on their platform

FEE #2: PLATFORM TRANSACTION FEE (their cut of every sale)
  • Glofox/Mindbody take an additional 2–2.5% of every payment
  • This is THEIR fee — on top of credit card processing
  • At $25K/month revenue, that's ~$500–$625/month going to THEM

FEE #3: CREDIT CARD PROCESSING (Stripe — everyone pays this)
  • Stripe charges 2.9% + 30¢ per transaction to process the card
  • This is unavoidable — every business pays this no matter what
  • At $25K/month, that's ~$725/month
  • This fee is the SAME whether you use Glofox, me, or anyone

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

SIDE-BY-SIDE AT $25K/MONTH REVENUE:
